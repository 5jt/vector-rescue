## Programme for February Meeting

Date: 8th February at 2.00pm<br>
Venue: The Gilbert Room,<br>
Institute of Electrical Engineers<br>
Savoy Place, London.

### Outstanding Achievement Award Presentation

This year’s Outstanding Achievement Award was awarded to Metapraxis of Kingston, Surrey for their world-renowned Management Information system ‘Resolve’. At this months meeting, David Preedy (of Metapraxis) will receive the award, and he will follow with a full demonstration of **Resolve**.

The remainder of the meeting will be taken up by a talk from Paul Gallagher (British Airways) on a PC-based APL application from British Airways that looks into “long term strategic planning in the modern world”.

## Vendors’ Forum

Date: 26th April at 2.00pm<br>
Venue: as above

For the last two years, we have reserved a meeting for vendors of APL interpreters to come along and tell us about the new “goodies” they have in store for us. This has proved very successful, so this year we continue the tradition.
