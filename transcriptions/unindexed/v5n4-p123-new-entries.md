*Compiled by Alison Chatterton*
{ .byline }

Apologies to all those people who have sent in requests for disks. Although the intention was that I should take over the software library from Dave Ziemann in January, other commitments and a missing piece of equipment, have meant that this has only just occurred - mid-April '89.

I hope that by the time you read this, many of your requests will have been cleared.

A short catalogue of all available disks including I-APL, Morten Kromberg's GRAF, the FinnAPL statistics and Zdenek V. Jizba's tutorials will be printed in Vector 6.1

In addition, a number of new disks, which may be of interest, will have been added to the library. These include:

- **APL tools for building expert systems. Donor: Z.Jizba**
- **APL kits. Donor: F.Espinasse**
- **Lotus menus. Donor: A.Wiggins**
- **APL migrate. Donor: R.Naugle**
- **PortaAPL demo.Donor: R.Naugle**
