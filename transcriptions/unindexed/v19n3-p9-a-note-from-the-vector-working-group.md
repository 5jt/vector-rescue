## A note from the Vector Working Group

From time to time, in the spirit of free exchange of ideas within a friendly and informal community, VECTOR publishes candid comments by individual BAA members, without them necessarily representing the view of the editorial team as a whole. Nevertheless we would like to offer our regrets over the offence which Dr. Clark’s remarks undoubtedly gave to Professor Alfonseca and the organisers of the APL 2002 Madrid conference, remarks which Dr Clark has since chosen to retract.
