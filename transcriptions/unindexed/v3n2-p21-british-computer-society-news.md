## BCS Bulletin

The BAA Chairman has received the following letter from David Knight, the review editor of BCS Bulletin.

> "I am writing to ask for your help in reviewing books for the BCS Bulletin. Over many years we have built up a large index file of potential reviewers, which is unfortunately thoroughly out of date.
>
> "Many Specialist Group Chairmen have been kind enough to help by offering to take books and distribute them to interested Group members. It would be very useful if this concept could be extended to include more Groups. Otherwise, could you please canvass your membership to see who would be willing to have their names placed on the reviewers list?"

Any BAA members who would like to be involved in this activity should contact David Knight at the following address:

> Computer Centre,<br>
> University College London,<br>
> Gower Street,<br>
> London,<br>
> WC1E 6BT.
