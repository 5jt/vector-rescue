### Nominations for the British APL Association Committee

The next AGM must be held in May 1997 and, seen from this side of Christmas, that seems a long way off. However, there are just two issues of Vector between now and then. Here, therefore, is an appeal for people to serve on the Committee for the year beginning next May.

Please persuade and nominate your friends or colleagues and we shall be happy to publish details of their candidature in the January issue of Vector. The election will be held in April by postal ballot. Anyone who would like to be given a feel for what is expected of the various Committee members should contact someone on the current Committee who would be delighted to receive any expression of interest.

Please send your nominations to the Secretary.
