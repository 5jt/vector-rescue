*2:00 p.m. Friday 23rd May 2003*<br>
*Royal Statistical Society, Errol Street, London EC1Y 8LX*

**Agenda:** Minutes, Chairman’s report, Treasurer’s report, Questions to Committee, Appointment of Auditor and Other Business. A member who wishes to raise other business should give a month’s notice to the secretary.

Nominations are invited for the Committee to take office after the AGM. Nominees must agree, be proposed and seconded by members, and be notified to the secretary by March 31<sup>st</sup> 2003. If an election is needed it will be held by post.

Anthony Camacho, Hon. Sec. January 2003
