*2.00 p.m. Friday 24th May 2002*<br>
*Royal Statistical Society, Errol Street, London EC1Y 8LX*

### Agenda

1. Minutes of Annual General Meeting 2001
2. Chairman’s Report
3. Subscriptions
4. Audited Statement of Accounts – Treasurer’s report
5. Questions to Committee
6. Appointment of Auditor
7. Other business

Other business cannot be discussed without giving notice and may require that a notice is sent to members. If you wish to raise a matter of other business, please let the secretary know by 31st March 2002 at the latest.

Anthony Camacho, Hon Sec, January 2002

### Vendor Presentations

There will be a full programme of Vendor presentations immediately following the AGM, with a teabreak around 3:00. MicroAPL and Dyadic Systems will definitely be among those presenting the latest features of their APL systems.

### Committee Nominations for 2002-2003

Nominations are invited for next year’s committee, to hold office from 1st May 2002 to 30th April 2003. Please send the name of the nominee (who must have consented to stand), the proposer and the seconder (all must be members of the Association) to the secretary, Anthony Camacho, [address omitted] or by email to [e-mail address omitted]. Nominations must be in the secretary’s hands before the 31st March 2002. If there is more than one nomination for any post there will be a postal election.
