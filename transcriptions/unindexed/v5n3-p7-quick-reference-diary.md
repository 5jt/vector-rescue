The preparations for APL90 are progressing well and we hope to see you all on August 13-17 1990 in Denmark. Now may be a good time to start thinking about the paper(s) you will submit: the official call for papers will be issued shortly after the summer holidays.

In order to provide the optimal setting for the conference, it has been finally placed in Elsinore (the city of Hamlet) just north of Copenhagen. This choice offers very attractive surroundings, improved conference facilities and a smaller conference fee. Should you decide to bring the family, the conference hotels offer excellent family accommodation.
