| 4th - 8th August 1991 | Stanford | APL91: The Next 25 Years |

British APL Association meetings are to be held at the Royal Over-Seas League, Park Place, near Green Park Tube station. Meetings begin at 2.00pm and close at 5.30pm.

Meetings are normally held on the 3rd Friday of the month throughout the autumn and spring. Dates for the 1991 programme will be confirmed at the September committee meeting.
