by Dave Ziemann
{ .byline }

The lateness of VECTOR 2.3 seriously reduced the number of entries to this ‘bumper sticker’ competition. This had the fortunate effect of making the judging a lot easier. Twenty five little bromides were boiled down to three winners; Martyn Adams supplied

> The longer the function the shorter my patience.

While Claude Henriod from Switzerland gives the following yardstick:

> The number of parentheses in an APL statement is inversely proportional to the experience of the programmer.

The first prize goes to Zeke Hoskins from Canada, who warns us that

> A good language with a bad editor is a bad language.

Surely he can’t be thinking of APL can he? £25, £15 and £10 (or equivalent) will be sent to Zeke, Claude and Martyn.
