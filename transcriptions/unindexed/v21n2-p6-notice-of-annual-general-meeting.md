## British APL Association AGM 2005 to be held at the Royal Statistical Society starting at 2 pm on Friday 20<sup>th</sup> May 2005

### Agenda

1. Minutes of AGM 2004 (as published in Vector)
2. Report from the Chairman (Adrian Smith)
3. Report from the Treasurer (Nicholas Small)<br>(including report from membership secretary)
4. Committee for 2005-2006<br>Nominations by 30 April, please. The Committee welcomes people who would like to join the committee; details from the secretary or any member of the committee.
5. Appointment of Auditor
6. Questions
7. Any other business

Anthony Camacho, Hon Sec

**Following the AGM there will be a Dyalog masterclass on object oriented programming starting at approximately 2.30pm.**
