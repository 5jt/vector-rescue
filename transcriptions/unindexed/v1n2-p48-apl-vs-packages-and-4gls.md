*May 18th 1984, Imperial College, London*

Notes compiled by Adrian Smith
{ .byline }

In all 48 people signed the register for this meeting, which consisted of two talks and a panel discussion. The speakers were Dominic Murphy and Des Booth, and the panellists were:

- Des Booth (APL\*PLUS)
- Sylvia Camacho (Tesco)
- Dominic Murphy (IPL)
- Chris Miles (Independent Consultant)
- Dave Wetherby (IPSA)

I have in my possession a tape of the entire discussion, but unfortunately the only audible word is an unattributed comment of ‘Knickers!’. However Des Booth has come up trumps with a full text, so the meeting will not go entirely unrecorded for posterity.
