The intention of this section is to bring some of the activities and publications of the British Computer Society to the attention of British APL Association members who may not otherwise receive such information.

Further information about the BCS (including membership application) is available from The British Computer Society; 13 Mansfield Street; London W1M 0BP.

## Publications:

*Software Engineering Journal* – a new journal, published jointly with the IEE, first issue due early in 1986, six issues a year at an annual subscription rate of £17 for BCS members (£47 for non-members, and £15 for students). ‘The Editors welcome papers’. Free sample copies will be available from IEE; PO Box 8; Southgate House; Stevenage; Herts SG1 1HQ.
