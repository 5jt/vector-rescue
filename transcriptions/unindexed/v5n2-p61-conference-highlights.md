*by Adrian Smith*
{ .byline }

## Michael Wheatley on the History of the APL Environment

A lot of really good hard 'inside information' on the early development of APL, and how it fitted in to its environment. The rationale behind the development of `⎕NA` and the shared variable processors.

## John Scholes on the Dyalog Session Manager

A really good idea, well implemented and well demonstrated. Thoroughly enjoyable.

## The APL\*PLUS II Product Forum

Again, lots of good hard information from the man who knows more about it than most. Invaluable for anyone thinking of moving to 32-bit APL.

## Hands on IBM's 32-bit APL2/PC

A product destined to put the wind up STSC. A real joy to play with; I can't wait to get my hands on a review copy.
