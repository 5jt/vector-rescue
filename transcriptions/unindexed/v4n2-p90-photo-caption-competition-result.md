by Dave Ziemann
{ .byline }

Apologies to those of you who have been patiently awaiting the result of this competition; for one reason or another it got held over from one issue of Vector to the next, to the next, …etc.

The four photographs labelled A, B, C and D were taken at APL86 and readers were invited to supply suitable captions. Sam Sexton suggested that photograph A should read:

A
: “You certainly need these rose-tinted glasses for looking at APL2!”

Sam’s entry was submitted on an I P Sharp Associates compliment slip. Iain Hunneybell thought that photo C should be captioned with:

C
: “Speak no evil, Hear no evil, See no evil.”

Gareth Brentnall submitted the following three entries

A
: “I didn’t think these new-fangled graphics would help me work out what dyadic transpose did on a 3-D array!

C
: “God, is it encode or decode? I hope he doesn’t ask me.”

D
: “I knew rotate extended to tables, but chairs?!”

Unfortunately, there were no printable entries for photograph B. Sam and Iain each receive fourteen pounds, and Gareth wins a star prize of twenty-two pounds.
