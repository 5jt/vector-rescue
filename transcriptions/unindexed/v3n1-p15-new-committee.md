## Annual General Meeting - initial report

*23rd May 1986, Royal Overseas League, London*

At the AGM, the outgoing committee proposed that two new committee officers be introduced for the coming year. The two new posts are Recruitment Officer and Projects Officer. The Recruitment Officer will be responsible for initiating an active campaign to encourage wider Association membership. The Projects Officer will create a series to new projects to increase awareness and use of APL and enhance the reputation of the Association, building on the success of VECTOR and the anticipated success of APL86.

In the subsequent committee elections the following members were elected:

and David Eastwood was declared elected.

A full report of the AGM, including the Chairman’s and Treasurer’s reports will appear in the next issue of VECTOR.
