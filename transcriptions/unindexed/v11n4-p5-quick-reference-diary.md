At the Committee meeting of 10 February 1995 it was decided that there was insufficient time and effort available to organize a Beginners Workshop for 25 March. Any such exercise cannot hope to succeed without considerable advance publicity as it must attract support from outside the established APL community. However, it was agreed that the Workshop held at the end of January 1995 should be repeated in October 1995. Please watch this space for details.

*Please note that we have been unable to obtain the usual venue for the meeting of 19th May 1995 but speakers are being arranged for a replacement meeting on 7th July 1995. Details will be mailed as usual.*

There will be an AGM on 19th May 1995 as announced but, on this occasion, without any following speakers. All members will have received a mailing of venue and Agenda and are welcome to attend.

British APL Association meetings are normally held in the IEE, Savoy Place. Nearest tube outlets: Temple or Embankment.
