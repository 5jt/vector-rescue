*Royal Statistical Society, Errol Street, London EC1Y 8LX*

### Agenda

1. Minutes of Annual General Meeting 2000
2. Chairman’s Report
3. Proposal to increase subscriptions
4. Audited Statement of Accounts – Treasurer’s report
5. Questions to Committee
6. Appointment of Auditor
7. Other business

Other business cannot be discussed without giving notice and may require that a notice is sent to members. If you wish to raise a matter of other business, please let the secretary know by 31<sup>st</sup> March 2001 at the latest.

Anthony Camacho, Hon Sec, December 2000

## Nominations for the Committee for 2001-2002

Nominations are invited for next year’s committee, to hold office from 1st May 2001 to 31st April 2002. Please send the name of the nominee (who must have consented to stand), the proposer and the seconder (all must be members of the Association) to the secretary, Anthony Camacho, [address omitted] or by email to [e-mail address omitted]. Nominations must be in the secretary’s hands before the 31st March 2001. If there is more than one nomination for any post there will be a postal election.
