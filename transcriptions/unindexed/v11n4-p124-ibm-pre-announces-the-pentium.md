In “*The APL Handbook of Techniques*” on page 52, it says

> B = 222333333344444445555555<br>
> C = 12345.67890123456789
>
> So (B \* C)/B =<br>
> &nbsp;&nbsp;12345.678901234567889994910

There is only one way to get such inaccuracy, and how did IBM know about it 15 years ago?
