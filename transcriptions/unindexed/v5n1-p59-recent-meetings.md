## Recent Meetings

This section of VECTOR includes notes on a number of BAA meetings which have taken place during 1988.

I would like to thank the speakers for making available such a complete text of their talk; many members find it hard to attend as many London meetings as they would like, and this section of VECTOR provides a valuable service to them.
