This issue’s contributed articles concentrate on different aspects of mainframe APL.

Mark Lenihan, of Natwest Bank, offers a valuable history of how they are handling the transition from VSAPL to APL2. We hope they will soon be able to tell us about their experiences using the new facilities of APL2 and the benefits it has given them.

For those users still running VSAPL under TSO, David Piper – a consultant with Cocking & Drury – offers some practical suggestions in response to some of the points raised by Adrian Smith a couple of issues ago.
