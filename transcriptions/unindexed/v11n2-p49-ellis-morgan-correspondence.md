*From: Ellis Morgan, 25 July 1994*
{ .byline }

I am writing to let you know how valuable I found last week’s *APL in Business at Swansea*.

It was the first conference I have been to since I started working for myself, so I was apprehensive about both the cost and the week of lost earnings. I now feel that the time and money were well spent.

I am sure that the chance to see how others are using the extended capability of APL in today’s desktop and client/server environments, the hands on training and practice in accessing windows resources and the software you distributed will add to the quality of my work by widening my repertoire while saving me time.

Thank you both for organising such a worthwhile and enjoyable conference. Thanks also to all the people at Swansea who looked after us so well and to the contributors, particularly those who put so much effort into the workshops.
