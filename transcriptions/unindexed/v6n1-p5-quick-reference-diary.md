British APL Association meetings are to be held at the Royal Over-Seas League, Park Place, near Green Park Tube station. Meetings begin at 2.00pm and close at 5.30pm.

## Topics for Forthcoming BAA Meetings

*by Peter Branson*
{ .byline }

- Design of large APL systems. Considerations of workspace structure; function modularity; data structures; file systems; end-user screens etc. (Illustrated with practical applications.)
- APL at British Airways. A review of some of their major applications using APL. (This meeting will be held at the BA headquarters at Heathrow Airport, where a full demonstration of the systems is possible.)
