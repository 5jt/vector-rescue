Thanks to Adin Falkoff for the following comments: "The serifs are too large on a number of upper-case characters, especially the bottom of the *F*, the verticals on the *E*, the horizontals on *H* and *W*, and the tops of *P* and *E*; the upper slant on the *K* breaks too sharply where it meets the slant leg; the curl at the bottom of the *J* should have a slightly more bulbous end; the right half of the squiggle on the *Q* is too angular, as is the flourish on the bottom of the ⍺; the top of the ∊ is perhaps a little flattened, or the bar is too high; and the slant of the numeral 1 should have a slight concavity, similar to the arrow heads, which are good. The special APL characters are generally good; my only quibble there is that the ⎕ is a little narrow for its height." *Will be fixed for 7.1! Keep ’em coming … Ed.*
