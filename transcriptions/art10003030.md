*David Eastwood (MicroAPL Ltd)*; notes by Adrian Smith
{ .byline }

It was now a case of splitting into manageable groups, and forming a series of loose scrums around the available screens! The quality of my notes degenerates badly at this point . . . sorry!

GDDM-68000 is a straight emulation of GDDM 3.0 and AP126. It is written in 68000 Assembler, and looked very responsive on MicroAPL’s Aurora. It takes around 50K off your free memory (not a lot, considering what it does), and as far as I could see it really does look just like GDDM. M. Legrand would be proud of them (see reviews!).
