APL Product Guide - Updates

compiled by Gill Smith

Vector 13:2, page 41

implementations applications APL community

Transcribed from the printed issue; not yet reviewed. Read it in the PDF of the issue, page 41

VECTOR's exclusive Product Guide aims to provide readers with useful information about sources of APL hardware, software and services. We also welcome information on APL clubs and groups throughout the world.

Getting Ready for the Web¶

The Vector Web site already contains a full upload of the existing product guide, from Vector 13.1, with the updates shown in the following few pages. We want to get the information as current and complete as we can, and it would be good to maintain the on-line copy as soon as new information becomes available. To prepare for this, we shall shortly be mailing everyone on the address list asking for updates and confirmation of the current entry. If you don't reply, we will assume you no longer exist, and your entry will be removed.

Updating your Entry¶

You will soon be able to mail in your updates using the form on the web site, but you can still send material by traditional email or by post to the address below.
