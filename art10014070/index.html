APL2001, Yale
The annual conference for 2001 is being held at Yale University (which is celebrating its 300th anniversary). For further details please see the flyer or visit the conference website at www.acm.org/sigapl.

J User Conference 2001
For details of the J User Conference please see the JSoftware website at www.jsoftware.com or email Anne Faust.

APL 2000 User Conference
For details please visit APL2000’s website at www.apl2000.com/con01.htm.

APL2002, Madrid
The annual conference for 2002 will be held at the Universidad Autonoma de Madrid, Spain. Professor Manuel Alfonseca is the Conference Chairman, and details will be posted on the website www.apl2002.com as they become available.
