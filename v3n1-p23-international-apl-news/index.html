Compiled by David Preedy

Vector 3:1, page 23

APL community

Transcribed from the printed issue; not yet reviewed. Read it in the PDF of the issue, page 23

APLBUG¶

We have received two recent issues of the APLBUG newsletter with brief papers as well as reports on their recent meetings. The March newsletter contains a solution to the Loss Carry-Forward problem raised in VECTOR Vol.2, No. 1. Tibor Horvath’s function is reprinted in the “Surely There Must Be A Better Way” column. The April issue reports on “Object Oriented Programming in APL” and how to overcome date problems for the year 2000 – and for the next 2 billion years provided the Earth’s rotation is unchanged. APLBUG membership costs $15 and applications should be sent to Curtis A Jones (see address list below).

APL Quote-Quad¶

Volume 16/3 of Quote-Quad contains a mix of notices, product news, articles, reviews, algorithms and recreation. The articles cover a Boolean Array Based Algorithm, Emulating Prolog, Trees and Networks using APL2, and an APL Workshop. Orders for APL Quote-Quad should be placed with:

Fred Aronson, Coordinator for SIGS,

USA 10036.
