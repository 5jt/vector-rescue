Comparison with the archive

Written 11 October 2026, comparing archive.vector.org.uk with this development build.

archive.vector.org.uk is the restored PHP site that served Vector online until 2016: the editors’ index, and the articles published as web pages. This build starts from the same index and the same web pages, and adds the printed issues. This page reviews what differs between the two, article by article.

In short¶

  • Coverage. The archive shows text for 630 of the 1,499 records in its index; this site has text for 1,430 of its 1,503. The archive’s own full index links only 661 articles; the other 838 records have an empty page there. This site gives every record a page, and lists everything on every issue’s Contents page (2,553 items), not only what the editors indexed.
  • Where both have text (620 articles), the words agree exactly in 460. Most of the rest are APL characters: in 89 articles the archive shows the bytes of a pre-Unicode APL font (© for ⍝, „ for ←), where this site shows the APL.
  • The archive is better in no respect now found: the one line of code our converter had damaged is mended (below).
  • Scans. The archive offers the PDFs of 12 issues (21:4, 23:4, the Dyalog at 25 supplement and Volumes 24–26) and 24 articles’ own PDFs. This site links a scan of 97 issues (all but 2:2, of which no copy has been found, and 3:4, 7:4 and 16:4, whose only copies are damaged) and the same 24 article PDFs, each Contents line to its page.

The index¶

Both sites use the editors’ index (index.xml). The archive has 1,499 records; this site has 1,503, adding the four articles published online in 2016–17 after the archive was last updated (Conquering recursion, Dyalog ’16 – an insider’s view, Larger than life automata, Minutes of the BAA AGM 2017).

The index was compiled by hand. This site corrects it from the printed Contents pages: 84 corrections to 76 records (pages, issues, authors and titles), each listed in the build report. Four records index an article a second time; on this site the second address leads to the first (for example 10013600, indexed as “Review of Pocket APL”, is the APL2002 conference report 10000300). One, 10012820, indexed as the 11:1 APL94 Abstracts, is the Update on APL94 printed in 10:4, and is filed there.

Where both have text¶

The 620 articles converted from the same web pages were compared word for word with the archive’s rendering of them (1,085,526 words), leaving aside headings, bylines and this site’s subject tags.

difference articles words
none 460
APL in a pre-Unicode font: the archive shows the font’s bytes 89 3,606
a web page shared by several articles: the archive shows the whole page 12 10,949
text in angle brackets, lost at the archive 13 105

other (mostly mis-decoded quotes, and wording around headings) 75 754

APL characters. Before Unicode, Vector’s web pages set APL in fonts that put APL symbols at the codes of ordinary characters. The archive shows those codes as they are, under a warning that “this article might contain pre-Unicode character-mapped APL code”: in 10000730 headings„'Region' is headings←'Region' and every ∇ is ’; elsewhere © stands for ⍝, › for ⊂, ‡ for ↓, ª for ⋄. This site maps each font back to Unicode (10000730), with per-article corrections where a mapping alone was not enough.

Shared pages. The letters of 22:2 and 22:4 and the Sustaining Members’ news of 23:1 were each published as one web page, which the index gives to several records. The archive shows the whole page under each title; this site shows each record its own part (10011240, Kenecdotes, against the archive’s page, which begins with another letter).

Angle brackets. Text such as <wsid>, <ctrl+break> or <function 1> is read by the archive as an HTML tag and disappears: “loadws=” at the archive is “loadws=<wsid>” here (10001980).

Encoding. Where a web page’s encoding was mis-declared, the archive shows � for curly quotes and accented letters (“Let�s”, 10004060); this site decodes them.

Stray markup. In four articles the archive shows fragments of the web page’s own header as text: ml version="1.0" encoding="utf-8"?> Sustaining Members' News | Vector 21.2 body {max-width:40em} (10014210) or YPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 3.2//EN"> (10003640).

Web addresses. Links that Vector printed to archive.vector.org.uk appear at the archive rewritten to britishaplassociation.co.uk; this site keeps them as published, and serves the old /art<ID> addresses itself.

Where the archive was right and this site wrong. One line of code in the report of the San Quirico Moot (23:3): the source has ⍝ <-----3----->, the archive shows it so, and our converter dropped three hyphens, taking them for the end of an HTML comment. Mended (#283).

Method¶

The archive’s full index and its page for each of the 661 articles it links were fetched on 8 October 2026 (sources/php-site/); on 11 October the live index was checked and links the same 661 articles, and pages for a sample of unlinked records were fetched and found empty. The comparison of words uses the build’s check (vecrescue fetch-php --check), extended to list every difference rather than a sample, and leaves out headings, bylines, subject tags and the archive’s hidden debug log. Counts of articles in the table of differences overlap where an article has differences of more than one kind.
