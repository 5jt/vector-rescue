*Updated 6 October 2026, after Volume 4.*

## What this is

A development build of the recovered *Vector* archive: every issue’s contents, every surviving article, and the old article addresses (`/art10000000`) kept working. The full index and the volume pages list every item on each issue’s printed Contents page, linked to the best text we have.

## Done

- **Volumes 22–26 and the online-only articles**: converted from the text that was published online (630 articles).
- **The First Pass through Volumes 1–4** (1984–1988), from the scanned issues:
    - every article in the editors’ index that survives in a scan transcribed from the page images: 111 of the 132 (one more was online as text; the other 20 are in 2:2, of which no copy has been found, and 3:4, of which only machine-read text survives). One, XPL, could not be transcribed and awaits a human transcriber. Printed results were recomputed wherever possible, and doubts recorded for review;
    - the pieces the index omitted from the meetings, general and technical sections also transcribed (155 pieces), and published;
    - the front sections (editorials, news, product guides, reviews) listed from the Contents pages and linked to their pages in the issue PDF.
- **Every other issue**: its articles listed from the index, linked to the issue PDF (with its machine-read text) where we have one.
- **Subject tags** for the transcribed articles of Volumes 1–4, from a vocabulary of 20 subjects; see [Tags](../tags/).
- **Damaged copies**: for three issues whose only copy is truncated (3:4, 7:4, 16:4), the machine-read text has been recovered and shown.

## Remaining

- **The First Pass through Volumes 5–21**: transcribe the articles in the editors’ index from the scans; list the rest of each Contents page and link it to the PDF.
- **Subject tags** for the articles transcribed from Volume 5 on, extending the vocabulary as needed.
- **Review**: every transcription is a draft until a reviewer checks it against the scan; see the [review checklist](https://github.com/5jt/vector-rescue/blob/main/plans/pdf-review-checklist.md).
- **Missing sources**: no scan has been found of 2:2 or 7:2, and the copies of 3:4, 7:4 and 16:4 are damaged ([help find them](https://github.com/5jt/vector-rescue/issues/62)).
- **A Second Pass**, if resources allow: transcribe what the First Pass only links to the PDF.

The quality of each article’s text is shown in the [full index](../full-index/).
